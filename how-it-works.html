From idea to launch, step by step.

No confusion and no surprises. Here’s exactly what happens when you work with A.J-Dev.

Tell us about your idea

Fill in the short form on the Get Started page, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us about your business, what you need and when you need it.

We plan and send a quote

We go through your goals, suggest the pages and features that fit, and send a clear quote and timeline before any work starts.

Design

We shape the look and feel around your brand: colors, layout and content that suit your customers.

Build

We code your site to be fast, responsive and easy to use on phones, tablets and computers.

Review and refine

You see the site before it goes live. We adjust it until you’re happy with it.

Launch and support

We put your site online and stay available afterwards for updates, fixes and maintenance.

Common questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the project. A simple landing page is much quicker than a full online store. We agree on a timeline with you in the quote.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the pages and features you need. Send us your idea through the Get Started page and we’ll give you a free quote.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is made to work well on phones, tablets and computers.

Can you update my site after it’s live?

Yes. We offer maintenance and updates after launch, so your site stays current and keeps working well.
